CSR Funding Proposal

Project Name: Solar-Powered Rural Health Outposts
Submitted by: Grameen Swasthya Trust
Region: Bundelkhand
Domain: Healthcare
Description: Establishes three solar-powered health outposts in villages currently over 8km from the nearest functioning clinic, providing basic diagnostics, maternal care, and a stocked pharmacy staffed by a resident nurse.

Requested Budget: Rs. 14,00,000
Expected Beneficiaries: 2200

Background:
Bundelkhand's rural clusters west of the district headquarters have no health
infrastructure within a reasonable walking distance. The nearest primary
health centre is a two-hour journey on unpaved roads, which is especially
dangerous during monsoon season and for maternal emergencies.

Implementation Plan:
- Month 1-2: Site survey and solar equipment procurement
- Month 3-4: Outpost construction and staffing
- Month 5: Outposts operational, community outreach begins
